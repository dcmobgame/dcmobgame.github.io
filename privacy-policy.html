Privacy Policy

This policy explains how DoviGames collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our games and applications.

Effective date: August 4, 2026

1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to DoviGames applications, games, websites, and related services. “Personal data” means information that identifies you or can reasonably be linked to you. DoviGames determines why and how personal data covered by this policy is processed.

2. Information we collect

Depending on your device, permissions, location, and use of our services, we may collect:

  • advertising identifiers, including IDFA on iOS and GAID on Android;
  • device, operating system, language, region, application version, and technical diagnostic information;
  • gameplay activity, session timing, progress, interactions, and feature usage;
  • advertising activity, such as impressions, viewing time, and clicks;
  • information stored through cookies or similar local technologies; and
  • information you provide when contacting us.

3. Analytics and advertising partners

Our applications may include software development kits supplied by analytics, advertising, and platform partners. Partners referenced by our services may include GameAnalytics, MoPub, AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, Anzu, ironSource, Facebook, Tapjoy, Vungle, and Mintegral. These providers may process information under their own privacy policies and applicable law.

4. How we use information

We use collected information to:

  • provide, operate, secure, and support our applications;
  • understand use, troubleshoot problems, and improve the player experience;
  • develop new applications, content, and features;
  • measure application and advertising performance;
  • show advertising that may be more relevant to your interests, where permitted; and
  • respond to questions, support requests, and legal obligations.

5. Consent and legal basis

Where required, we ask for your consent before collecting or using information for analytics or personalized advertising. You may withdraw consent through application or device settings. Depending on the processing and your location, we may also rely on performance of a service, legitimate interests, or compliance with legal obligations.

6. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy. Unless a longer period is required for security, legal, or dispute-resolution reasons, covered application data is generally retained for no more than 13 months after collection. Data may then be deleted or anonymized for statistical use.

7. How information is shared

Information may be available to authorized DoviGames personnel and service providers that need it to perform work for us. Providers must process information for agreed purposes, follow applicable instructions, and protect its confidentiality and security.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, public authorities, safety or security needs, or in connection with a merger, restructuring, financing, or sale of all or part of our business.

8. Consent to Share Consumption Data with Apple

This section applies only to purchases made through Apple's App Store in our iOS applications and does not apply to purchases made through Google Play.

By using our iOS app and making in-app purchases through Apple's App Store, you consent to our sharing of data regarding your usage and consumption of purchased content with Apple, as part of our efforts to resolve refund requests. This information may include details about how you have accessed and interacted with the purchased content. The purpose of sharing this data is to help Apple make an informed decision regarding refund requests. We ensure that such data sharing is done in compliance with Apple's policies and only as necessary to process your requests.

9. International data transfers

Our providers may process information outside your country. When applicable law requires it, we use recognized safeguards for international transfers, such as adequacy decisions, approved contractual protections, or another lawful transfer mechanism.

10. Children's privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, or any higher minimum age required in their location, unless a service expressly states otherwise. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below the applicable age. If you believe a child provided information without appropriate permission, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where required.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • confirm whether we process your personal data and request access to it;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • request deletion or restriction of processing;
  • object to certain processing or withdraw consent;
  • receive eligible data in a portable format; and
  • complain to your local data-protection authority.

To protect users, we may need to verify your identity before completing a request. We aim to respond within the period required by applicable law.

12. Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Where legally required, we will notify affected users and authorities of qualifying data incidents.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our services, practices, or legal obligations. The effective date at the top identifies the current version. Please review this page periodically.

14. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at dcmobgame@gmail.com.
